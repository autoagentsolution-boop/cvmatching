## D. Negative Filters (DO NOT INCLUDE IN MARKETING BUCKET)

### D1. Sales Roles Masquerading as Marketing

#### "Sales Marketing Manager" / "Sales and Marketing Manager"
- **Why it's misclassified:** These are fundamentally SALES roles with a marketing component.
- **Confirming negative keywords:** "sales target," "sales pipeline," "quota," "sales team management," "field sales"
- **Vietnamese variant:** "Quản lý Bán hàng và Marketing" — nearly always sales-primary
- **Filter rule:** If "sales" appears as primary function before "marketing," classify as SALES, not marketing.

#### "Telesales / Marketing" or "Sales / Marketing"
- **Why it's misclassified:** Slash notation indicates the person will perform BOTH, but primary responsibility is sales.
- **Confirming negative keywords:** "close deals," "sales calls," "sales conversion," "customer acquisition (via direct sales)"
- **Filter rule:** Double roles with "sales" + "marketing" should default to SALES if job description emphasizes revenue/quota.

#### Sales Executive with Marketing Support
- **Example from research:** Some postings titled "Sales & Marketing Executive" or "International Sales and Marketing Manager" that are 80%+ sales responsibilities.
- **Filter rule:** Read job description; if >60% responsibility is sales/revenue, filter to SALES.

### D2. Brand Ambassador (Ambiguous: Often Sales, Not Marketing)

- **Why it's risky:** 39 brand ambassador jobs exist in Vietnam, but many are part-time event/retail roles with strong sales component.
- **Confirming negative keywords:** "commission-based," "incentive-based," "direct sales," "customer conversion," "merchandising," "point-of-sale support"
- **Confirming positive keywords (rare, but keep if present):** "brand education," "reputation management," "strategic partnerships," "brand voice"
- **Filter rule:** Brand Ambassador roles should be INCLUDED only if job description emphasizes brand strategy/education/representation; EXCLUDE if 70%+ is field sales or direct customer interaction for revenue.

### D3. Marketing Engineer / Product Marketing Engineer (Often Sales Tech)

- **Why it's ambiguous:** "Marketing Engineer" definitions vary globally. In Vietnam tech sector, this role sometimes sits within Sales Engineering, not Marketing.
- **Confirming negative keywords:** "sales enablement," "sales engineering," "deal support," "customer acquisition (technical)"
- **Confirming positive keywords:** "product positioning," "go-to-market strategy," "customer messaging," "product marketing"
- **Filter rule:** If job emphasizes sales support or customer acquisition for salespeople, classify as SALES ENGINEERING. If product positioning/messaging, classify as MARKETING.

### D4. PR Officer (Non-Communications)

- **Why it's misclassified:** "PR Officer" can be a generic admin role in some VN companies (e.g., office management, facilities).
- **Confirming negative keywords:** "office administration," "event logistics," "facilities management," "support staff"
- **Confirming positive keywords:** "public relations," "media relations," "press release," "communications," "reputation management"
- **Filter rule:** "PR Officer" alone is ambiguous; only include if job description explicitly mentions media/communications.

### D5. HR / Recruitment Roles (Not Marketing)

- **Why it's distinct:** "Recruitment Marketing" exists as a niche role, but most HR job postings are NOT marketing.
- **Filter rule:** Exclude all traditional HR, recruitment, payroll, benefits administration roles. Include only if specifically titled "Recruitment Marketing" or "Employer Branding" with marketing-specific responsibilities.

### D6. Creative Roles Outside Marketing

- **Why it's misclassified:** Graphic designers or video editors may work in IT, product design, or video production — not marketing.

  - MARKETING context: "marketing collateral," "campaign assets," "brand guidelines," "promotional materials"
  - NON-MARKETING context: "UI/UX design," "software interface," "product design," "internal tools," "documentary video," "IT infrastructure"
- **Filter rule:** Include creative roles only if job description explicitly mentions marketing materials, campaigns, or brand support.

## F. Unresolved Ambiguities & Edge Cases

1. **Marketing Analyst vs. Data Analyst vs. BI Analyst:**
   - Boundary with tech/data is fuzzy. Some VN companies classify "Marketing Analyst" under data team, others under marketing.
   - Recommendation: Include "Marketing Analyst" if job explicitly mentions marketing metrics/attribution; exclude if purely technical data work.

2. **"Marketing + Design" Roles:**
   - Some JDs combine designer + marketer duties (especially startups).
   - Recommendation: If primary focus is design execution for marketing campaigns, include. If UI/UX design, exclude.

3. **"Recruitment Marketing" / "Employer Branding":**
   - Niche role: marketing applied to recruitment.
   - Recommendation: Include if explicitly titled "Recruitment Marketing" or "Employer Branding"; exclude generic HR roles.

4. **Affiliate Marketing Networks:**
   - Affiliate managers may work for affiliate networks (primarily performance-driven) vs. in-house (brand-driven).
   - Recommendation: Both structures are valid marketing roles; include both.

5. **"Product Marketing Manager" vs. "Product Manager":**
   - Product Manager = product strategy (usually sits in product/tech org)
   - Product Marketing Manager = product messaging/positioning (marketing org)
   - Recommendation: Include Product Marketing Manager; exclude Product Manager unless job explicitly mentions marketing messaging.
