# OTHER (Catch-All) Role Taxonomy

**Scope:** All roles NOT in supported verticals (tech, marketing, finance, sales, ops). Includes non-tech engineering, healthcare, education, hospitality, agriculture, legal services, security, trades, and miscellaneous professional categories.

**vl24h Coverage:** 13 unknown slugs; maps all out-of-scope jobs. Fallback for any unmapped slug.

## E. Disambiguation Rules

### Engineer Role Clarity
The keyword "Kỹ sư" (Engineer) appears in both TECH and OTHER contexts. Disambiguation rules:

**→ TECH:**
- Software Engineer, Data Engineer, ML Engineer, DevOps Engineer
- Keywords: code, database, Python, Java, cloud, CI/CD

**→ OTHER (Non-Tech Engineering):**
- Mechanical Engineer, Electrical Engineer, Civil Engineer, Chemical Engineer
- Keywords: AutoCAD, machinery, construction, electrical systems, chemistry

**→ OPS:**
- Manufacturing Engineer (ops execution focus), Quality Engineer (operational QC)
- Keywords: production planning, shift management, manufacturing operations

### Technician vs. Specialist
- **Kỹ thuật viên (Technician)**: Hands-on, field-based, skilled labor → Usually **OTHER**
- **Chuyên viên (Specialist)**: Professional/analytical role, may be OPS or OTHER depending on domain
- Rule: If hands-on, manual work, tools/machines → **OTHER (Trades)**. If professional role → check domain.

### Chef vs. Cook
- **Bếp trưởng (Head Chef/Executive Chef)**: Management/leadership role → **OTHER (Hospitality)**
- **Đầu bếp (Chef/Cook)**: Hands-on cooking role → **OTHER (Hospitality)** or **OPS (if manufacturing food)**
- Rule: Fine-dining or restaurant context → **OTHER**. Manufacturing food facility (shift work) → **OPS**.

### Designer Role Clarity
- **Graphic Designer (advertising, brand, campaign)** → MARKETING
- **Graphic Designer (packaging, product, UX)** → OTHER (Design)
- **Interior Designer** → OTHER (Architecture & Design)
- Rule: Check company context. Ad agency = MARKETING. Design firm or product company = **OTHER**.

### Trainer Role Clarity
- **Chuyên viên Đào tạo (L&D Trainer within HR)** → OPS
- **Giáo viên (Teacher in school/university)** → OTHER (Education)
- **Trainer (Corporate/External)** → Could be OPS (internal L&D) or OTHER (education/training center)
- Rule: If title includes "Giáo viên", "Giảng viên", or school/university context → **OTHER**. If HR-internal → **OPS**.

## F. Edge Cases & Confidence Notes

1. **Content Creator**: Brand/marketing content → MARKETING. Entertainment/performance content → **OTHER**.
2. **Security**: Cybersecurity (IT-focused) → TECH. Physical security → **OTHER (Security)**.
3. **Pharmacy**: Retail pharmacist = **OTHER (Pharma)**. Hospital pharmacist = **OTHER (Healthcare)**. Operational pharmacy role (inventory, distribution) = **OPS**.
4. **Skilled Trades**: Manufacturing context with shift work → **OPS**. Independent tradesman (electrician, plumber, welder) → **OTHER (Trades)**.
5. **Food Production**: Manufacturing facility (QC, production planning) = **OPS**. Food technologist (R&D, food science) = **OTHER (Food Science)**.
